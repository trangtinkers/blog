This talk is essentially an update on "The Wealth of Networks" which Benkler published back in 2006.

# Summary

- You can't escape systems or find the one system that guarantees freedom.
- Freedom is the capacity to move between systems with different constraints and affordances.
- He calls this something like **"freedom to bob and weave"**—being able to navigate between different institutional environments, exploit their strengths, avoid their failures, and adapt as circumstances change.
- **Don't look for the final solution. Build alternatives, keep systems open, criticize them, move between them, and continually update them according to your underlying values.**
	- *Popper's criterion*. Good political institutions are those that make it as easy as possible to detect whether a ruler or policy is a mistake, and to remove rulers or policies without violence when they are.
- Given that every system is going to be partially wrong, how do we maintain enough freedom and institutional diversity that people can navigate, resist, learn, and adapt?
# Highlights

> Freedom then becomes freedom to Bob and weave between different systems of constraint and affordance, different expectations of failure, different modes of failure and continuously updating and trying to go between these systems and harness them as we move move forward.

> Law where relevant in whatever form makes other systems make them open you have to be strategic you have to be systemic you have to be aware of the limitations and the context. You can't just assume one law is easily translatable or generalizable.

 > So to conclude, there is no single system to preserve Freedom. No fixed model or ideal guarantee of Freedom, not the market and the free market, not civil rights, not constitutional democracy. Each of these is not an abstract idea, it's an actually instantiated system with persistent and diverse imperfections that vary by context. Incompleteness, imperfection, continual practical updating and adaptation are a central feature of how we are to be free under conditions of complex modern societies typified by uncertainty unintended effects and change. Individual conscious therefore must play a critical constructive role perturbing systems trenchant criticism are gerine to the process of learning and Improvement in persistently imperfect system. You cannot just rely on the system.
