- ## **Weaving Social and Digital Technology Together** — why collective intelligence needs both

- ## **The Hum: Helping Organizations Decentralize** — culture over tech, the 80/20 workshop breakdown
    
    - **Rich:** Enspiral is a good case, because it fluctuates — I think we've had up to about three hundred people, and it always kind of condenses back; we're currently at around two hundred. It's focused on: okay, there are all these organizations that want to be decentralized — what's hard about it, and more importantly, how can we support them in getting over those challenges?
    - That's a really complex, nuanced question — how do you go into a context where people are trying to be self-organizing and offer them something that helps them self-organize better? It's really tricky. We've been doing this for eighteen months, in maybe twenty different countries — usually with at least one workshop per country involving twenty or thirty different organizations. So we're probably connected to somewhere around a thousand organizations trying to do this. It's very informal research — we're not doing science, but we're connecting with a lot of people and trying to understand what we can offer that will actually help.
    - If you look at one of our workshops, it's about 80% culture, 20% technology — and not just culture, but ideas too, a little dose of ideas, like how to think about power and leadership.
